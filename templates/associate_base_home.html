Common questions

Who is an "Associate" on Revolution HRMS?

An Associate is a consultant, accountant, or in-house HR professional who manages payroll and compliance for one or more companies from a single account.

Are you compliant with the New Labour Codes already?

Yes. Payroll and registers apply each company's state ruleset automatically — old Act or new Code — as states notify their rules, with no manual rework on your side.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade per company at any time. Billing adjusts from your next cycle.

Is my client data kept separate per company?

Yes. Every company's records, employees and registers are fully scoped and isolated, even within the same Associate account.
