We use only the cookies needed to run a secure payroll platform. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. This page forms part of our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser. They keep you signed in and help keep the site secure.

3. Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking necessary cookies will stop you from signing in.
