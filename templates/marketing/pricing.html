Frequently asked

Is pricing per employee or flat?

Flat monthly price per plan, up to the employee limit shown. Exceeding your slab prompts an upgrade — your data is never locked.

Do you offer offline / on-premise licensing?

Yes. A one-time lifetime license with annual support is available for factories needing on-premise deployment — contact sales for a quote.

What's included in Professional Tax coverage?

Copper through Gold cover single-state Professional Tax. Platinum adds the multi-state PT engine across 19+ Indian states, applied automatically by employee work location.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any renewal cycle without data migration.
