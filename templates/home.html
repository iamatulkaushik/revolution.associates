A payroll run

From punch to bank file

  1. Record registrationsPAN, TAN, EPF and ESI codes live on the company. Missing a registration means that deduction is blocked, not guessed.
  2. Capture attendanceFull day, half day, leave and overtime. A local sync daemon can pull biometric or RFID punches over your LAN, with LOP and overtime worked out from shifts.
  3. Run payrollEPF on Basic + DA, ESI by wage threshold, Professional Tax by state, LWF, TDS under old or new regime, loan EMIs and arrears.
  4. Generate filingsEPF ECR text file, ESI Excel upload, challans and statutory registers, as PDF, Excel or TXT.
  5. Pay and sharePayslips by PDF or bulk email. Bank upload files for HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis or a generic layout.
