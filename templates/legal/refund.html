2. Cancelling

  • Cancel any time by emailing {{ site_email }} from your registered email.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You keep access until then. No further charges are made.
  • Cancel at least [3] days before renewal so the next cycle is not billed.
  • Before cancelling, finish payroll runs and filings, download Form 16 and 24Q, and export your data. You can export for 30 days after the subscription ends. After that, data is deleted or anonymised as described in the Privacy Policy, subject to legal retention.

3. Changing plans

  • Upgrade: effective at once. We charge the difference [pro-rated for the rest of the cycle].
  • Downgrade: effective at next renewal. Features not on the lower plan stop from then. Your data is not deleted.
  • If you pass your plan's employee limit, we ask you to upgrade. Your data is not locked.

4. When we refund

  1. First-purchase refund: new paying customers may ask for a full refund of the first payment within [7] days of paying, if no live payroll, statutory filing or bank file has been generated.
  2. Billing errors: incorrect or double charges are refunded in full.
  3. Service failure: if a core feature you paid for is unavailable for more than [7] continuous days because of us and we cannot fix it, you may request a pro-rated refund for that period.
  4. Unauthorised payment: report it within 7 days of the charge.

Features marked "soon" or "planned" on the pricing page are not part of what you buy. Their delay is not a ground for refund unless you bought the plan on the written promise of a date.

5. When we do not refund

  • Partly used billing periods after the first-purchase window.
  • Downgrades or unused employee capacity.
  • Penalties, interest or losses from wrong inputs, approvals you gave, late filings, or the rules of EPFO, ESIC, tax or bank portals.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for breach of the Terms.
  • Government charges and payment-gateway fees, except where law requires a refund.

6. On-premise licence

The on-premise option is a one-time lifetime licence with annual support, priced by quotation. The licence fee is non-refundable once the software is delivered or activated, unless the quote says otherwise. Annual support is charged in advance and is non-refundable once the support year starts, except for billing errors. Without support renewal you may keep using your version but get no updates, including statutory rate and form changes.

7. How to ask

  1. Email {{ site_email }} from your registered email with company name, invoice number and reason.
  2. We acknowledge within 2 business days and review within 7.
  3. Approved refunds go to the original payment method within [7 to 10] business days. Banks may take longer. Where GST was charged we issue a credit note.
