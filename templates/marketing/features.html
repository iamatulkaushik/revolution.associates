Features

What is built, module by module

Each item shows its status. Nothing is listed as live unless it runs on a real payroll.

Statutory and tax

EPF and ESI live

EPF on Basic + DA with employer and employee split. ESI eligibility by wage threshold. ECR text file, ESI Excel upload and challans.

Professional Tax live

Slabs for 19+ states, applied by employee work location. LWF rules by state.

TDS and Form 16 live

Old and new regimes, investment declarations, Form 16 Part A and B, Form 24Q helper.

Pay and lifecycle

Loans and advances live

EMI schedule by instalment count or fixed amount, deducted until the balance is zero.

Arrears and increments live

Backdated changes recomputed, with statutory shortfall schedules.

Full and Final live

Leave encashment, notice pay, gratuity, loan and asset recovery, with certificates.

Attendance and shop floor

Biometric and RFID live

A local daemon relays punches from devices on your network. Shifts, overtime and loss of pay are worked out from them.

Registers live

Factory Act, Contract Labour, Bonus, Overtime, Fines and Minimum Wages registers.

Assets and expenses live

Track issued assets, deduct damages, route bills for approval, add tax-exempt amounts to payroll.

Payout and control

Bank files live

CSV or Excel for HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis and a generic layout.

Audit trail live

Records who changed what, when, and from which IP.

Maker-checker approval soon

HR prepares, the owner approves, then payslips and bank files unlock. Being rolled out.

Direct salary disbursement through a bank API is planned, not available.
